**What changed today**

The big focus was image quality and speed, since those two are where a portfolio site either earns trust or loses it.

- **Image pipeline.** Every photo now runs through a single optimization pipeline - resized to the right dimensions, compressed, EXIF stripped, and served in a format the browser loads fastest. When you upload through the admin panel, this happens automatically; nothing reaches the live site unoptimized.
- **Resolution.** I raised the masters the site serves, especially on the homepage and hero, so photos stay sharp on retina displays (MacBook, iPad, phones) rather than looking soft. The homepage tiles, hero, and project pages all pull higher-resolution versions now.
- **The grey flash on refresh.** You may have noticed grey boxes appearing for a moment when the page loads, before the photos fill in. That's gone - the site now pre-connects and prefetches the content at startup, so the first paint is already populated instead of empty.
- **Small polish.** Contact page photo now runs edge-to-edge to match the rest of the site (no side gutters there anymore). The footer Instagram icon's click area is fixed. A subtle scroll cue on the homepage hero. The principals on the About page are swapped so Shannon and Chris face the same direction. Nothing dramatic, just tightening.

The short version: the site is sharper and faster than it was yesterday, and the pieces are consistent with each other.
